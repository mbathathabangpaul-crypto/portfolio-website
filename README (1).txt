INSTALLATION
1. Back up your existing portfolio files.
2. Copy index.html into your project root. Copy style.css and app.js into your existing src/ folder, replacing the old files.
3. Keep your portrait at /images/thabang.jpg.
4. Keep your CV at assets/Thabang_Mbatha_CV.pdf.
5. Open index.html through your local development server.
